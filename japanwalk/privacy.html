Privacy Policy

JapanWalk

Last updated: November 25, 2026

JapanWalk is a walking guide for people visiting Japan on their own. Your location, voice, photos and anything you type stay on your iPhone. We run no servers, so we have no way to receive them. There are no accounts and no ads.

What the app uses on your iPhone

  • Location: to show nearby sights and draw your position and walking directions on the map. Directions are calculated on your iPhone from street data included in the app's downloads.
  • Camera: to read signs and menus, and to ask the guide what is in front of you.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to translate conversations and to ask the guide by voice. Speech is recognized on your iPhone with Apple's frameworks.
  • Translation and on-device AI: translation uses Apple's on-device translation, and the guide's answers are made on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. No outside AI service is used.
  • Photo library (add only): only when you save a photo you took.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your travel log: photos you showed the guide, its answers, and translated conversations
  • Your departure date, booking checkmarks and settings
  • Which areas you downloaded, which area you chose first, and whether you bought an area

All of this stays on your iPhone and the developer cannot see it. It is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or a computer) if you make them. The record of the first area you chose is kept in the iPhone keychain, so the free area is not reset by reinstalling the app.

When the app talks to Apple

Only in the cases below. Nothing is sent to the developer.

  • Area downloads: guide and street data for each area are downloaded from Apple's servers (Apple-hosted Background Assets) when you choose an area.
  • Map background: map images are loaded from Apple Maps, so the area you are viewing is shared with Apple. With no connection, the app still draws sights, your position and directions without the background.
  • Language data: the first time you use a language, Apple downloads the data for on-device translation and speech recognition.
  • Purchases: purchases and restores are handled by Apple's App Store. The developer only learns whether a purchase was made.
  • Official pages and Maps: only when you tap them, official pages open in an in-app browser, and a destination is passed to Apple Maps.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data on your iPhone. Nothing is kept on our side, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

Children

The app has no accounts, and the developer does not collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
