JapanWalk

JapanWalk is a walking guide for Tokyo and Kyoto that keeps working with no Wi-Fi or SIM: walking directions, guides to sights, sign reading and conversation translation all run on your iPhone.

Before your trip

  • Download the areas you plan to visit on Wi-Fi. Each area includes its guides and, for Tokyo, one shared street map for all six Tokyo areas.
  • The first area you choose is free to download. Other areas can be bought one by one.
  • Open the Translate tab once while you still have a connection, so your iPhone can download the language data it needs.

Common questions

  • Directions say I am outside the walking area. Each area covers the central streets you can walk. Take a train or subway closer, then try again.
  • Restoring a purchase. Open an area's purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Photo questions and conversation need a newer iPhone. The on-device guide uses Apple Intelligence and needs a supported iPhone running iOS 27 or later.
